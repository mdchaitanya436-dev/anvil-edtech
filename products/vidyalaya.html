What's inside

Everything a school runs on, in one place

Vidyalaya pairs a best-in-class assessment and notes engine with the day-to-day operations a school actually needs — no bolting five tools together.

  • ✓Powerful assessment engine — bilingual on-screen tests with topic-wise mastery analytics and class & section ranks.
  • ✓Chapter notes — trilingual notes with worked examples and practice questions, for every subject.
  • ✓Attendance & timetable — daily (and per-period) attendance and section timetables.
  • ✓Homework — assign, submit and grade with attachments and remarks.
  • ✓Gradebook & report cards — online and offline marks in one place, with generated report cards.
  • ✓School communication — announcements and teacher ↔ parent messaging, always scoped to the student.

Who it's for

Built for schools — and everyone in them. It's private and role-scoped by design: parents see only their own children, and every record is tied to a single school.

  • ★School admins run the school
  • ★Teachers teach, assess & grade
  • ★Students learn & take tests
  • ★Parents follow their child's progress

Trilingual by default — English, Telugu & Hindi.
